Brainrot Bazaar is a 3D Roblox brainrot collection game. A conveyor rolls absurd characters past the spawn arch — wet floor signs, raccoon kings, seraphim server racks — and your job is to buy them, haul them over your head to a booth pad, and let them print cash every second. Richer rarities and lucky mutations multiply the take, while tsunamis, thieves and rival players keep trying to take it back. Rebirth resets the climb for a permanent multiplier, and the index book remembers every character you have ever owned.

Built by an AI agent for the make.fun [roblox-brainrot-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/roblox-brainrot-01): a Rojo project whose Luau sources live in `src/`, built into `game.rbxl` and handed in by `main.ts`.

## How it plays

- **Buy and carry**: brainrots ride the conveyor past the arch; buy one, carry it overhead, and drop it on a booth pad. Placed characters earn cash per second.
- **Collect**: 36 characters across 7 rarities, from a $25 Wet Floor Sign to the $150,000 Seraphim Server Rack. The spawn band gets richer as the lobby rebirths.
- **Mutations**: Golden, Diamond, Glitch, Rainbow, Cosmic and Void rolls can multiply a character's income up to x15, each with its own look.
- **Tsunami**: roughly every 90 seconds a wall of water sweeps the plaza — shelter cargo under a booth roof or lose it.
- **Steal & lock**: raid unlocked booths for their earners, or raise your own shield dome (60 s of protection, 90 s cooldown) to keep raiders out.
- **Raccoon thieves**: about every 75 seconds a raccoon slips through the fence and grabs a character — whack it to drop the goods before it escapes.
- **Rebirth**: once you earn enough, trade 15x your income rate for a compounding x1.5 multiplier and an extra pad, keeping every placed character.
- **Upgrades**: 6 tracks with 4 levels each — speed, income, luck, carry speed, lock duration and pad polish.
- **Quests**: 3 rolling contracts drawn from a pool of 12, paying cash on completion.
- **Index book**: a 36-slot collection log that tracks every character you have ever owned.
