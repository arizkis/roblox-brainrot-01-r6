Brainrot Bazaar is a 3D Roblox brainrot collection game. Buy absurd characters off a moving conveyor, carry them to your booth, and let them earn cash every second — then defend the take from tsunamis, raccoon thieves and rival players, and rebirth to climb again with a permanent multiplier. 36 characters, 7 rarities, 6 mutations, and an index book that remembers everything you have ever owned.

Built by an AI agent for the make.fun [roblox-brainrot-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/roblox-brainrot-01): a Rojo project whose Luau sources live in `src/`, built into `game.rbxl` and handed in by `main.ts`.

## How it plays

- **Buy and carry**: brainrots ride the conveyor past the spawn arch; buy one, carry it overhead, and drop it on a booth pad. Placed characters earn cash per second.
- **Collect**: 36 characters across 7 rarities — Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, Divine — from a $25 Wet Floor Sign to the $150,000 Seraphim Server Rack. The spawn band shifts richer as the lobby rebirths.
- **Mutations**: 6 rolls — Golden, Diamond, Glitch, Rainbow, Cosmic, Void — multiply a character's income up to x15, each with its own look.
- **Tsunami**: every 90 seconds a wall of water sweeps the plaza; get cargo under a booth roof or lose it.
- **Steal & lock**: raid unlocked booths for their earners, or raise your own shield dome — 60 s of protection, 90 s cooldown.
- **Raccoon thieves**: about every 75 seconds a raccoon slips through the fence and grabs a character; whack it to drop the goods before it escapes.
- **Rebirth**: pay 15x your income rate for a compounding x1.5 multiplier and +1 pad, keeping every placed character.
- **Upgrades**: 6 tracks x 4 levels — speed, income, luck, carry speed, lock duration and pad polish.
- **Quests**: 12 contracts in the pool, 3 active at a time, paying cash on completion.
- **Index book**: a 36-slot collection log that tracks every character you have ever owned.
